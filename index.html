UNDERSTAND THE BASICS

Know what goes into your estimate.

These calculators use transparent assumptions so you can understand the numbers—not just the result.

01

Regular investing (SIP)

A systematic investment plan (SIP) invests a fixed amount at regular intervals. This estimate adds each contribution at month-end and applies monthly compounding. Market-linked returns are not guaranteed.

FV = P × [((1 + r)n − 1) / r]Use the SIP calculator →
02

Compound interest

Compound interest means earning returns on both your original principal and accumulated growth. This estimate assumes a constant annual rate compounded once per year.

A = P × (1 + r)tExplore compound interest →
