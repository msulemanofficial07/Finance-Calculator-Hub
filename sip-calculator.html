What is a systematic investment plan?

A systematic investment plan (SIP) is a way to invest a set amount at regular intervals, often monthly. Rather than choosing one large contribution date, an investor makes a series of smaller contributions. This calculator models that schedule; it does not recommend a particular investment or fund.

How to use this SIP calculator

Enter the amount you plan to contribute each month, an assumed annual rate of return and the number of years you expect to invest. Choose a display currency. The result updates as you change the inputs and separates total contributions from projected growth.

  • Monthly investment: the regular contribution entered for each month.
  • Annual return: a constant scenario assumption, not a forecast.
  • Investment period: the number of years contributions continue.

Formula and assumptions

The model applies the annual rate divided by 12, then adds each monthly contribution at the end of the month. For a positive monthly rate, the resulting future value is P × [((1 + r)n − 1) / r], where P is the contribution, r is the monthly rate and n is the number of contributions. At a zero rate, the result is simply P × n. Contributions are assumed to remain constant; fees, taxes and changing returns are excluded.

Interpreting a projection responsibly

Investment values can rise or fall, and actual returns may differ substantially from a smooth projection. Try more than one return assumption to see how sensitive the estimate is. Consider your goals, time horizon, costs, risk tolerance and local rules; speak with a qualified professional if you need personal advice.

Frequently asked questions

Are SIP returns guaranteed?

No. This calculator uses a constant rate you choose for illustration. Market returns vary and may be negative.

Does the estimate include fees or taxes?

No. Fees, taxes and other investment-specific charges are not included in this estimate.

Can I use this calculator outside India?

Yes. Select a display currency and enter values in that currency. The calculation is a general contribution-growth illustration, not country-specific investment or tax guidance.
