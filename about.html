ABOUT FINANCEHUB

Clear tools for exploring money questions.

FinanceHub is an independent educational website with simple calculators for exploring investment-growth assumptions, cryptocurrency trade outcomes and inflation. Our aim is to make the inputs and calculations understandable so visitors can compare scenarios for themselves.

How our calculators work

The calculators run in your browser and use the values you enter. They do not fetch live market prices, exchange rates or inflation data. Results are estimates based on stated assumptions; actual financial outcomes can differ.

We explain each tool's basic formula and limitations alongside the calculator. Please read those notes before relying on a projection. The calculators are not personalized financial, tax, legal or investment advice.

Editorial and commercial transparency

We link to selected public financial-education resources as independent references. Those external links are not affiliate links. If we later publish affiliate links or display advertising, we will identify commercial relationships and update the relevant disclosures and privacy information.

FinanceHub does not currently provide personalized financial advice or claim to be a licensed financial adviser. Check the credentials and local rules that apply before acting on financial information.
