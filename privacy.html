YOUR PRIVACY

Privacy Notice

Last updated: November 22, 2026

This notice describes the current FinanceHub website. Review and update it before adding advertising, analytics, forms, accounts or other data-collection services. Your web host may process technical request logs under its own settings and policies.

Calculator inputs

The calculator inputs are processed by JavaScript in your browser to display estimates. The current static site does not send calculator values to a FinanceHub server, create an account, or include a contact or lead form.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

The current site does not include analytics, advertising, affiliate tracking or cookie-based measurement scripts. It stores the selected display currency in browser local storage so the preference can be remembered across visits. This preference is not sent to FinanceHub as a financial record. If third-party services are added later, this notice will identify providers, purposes, controls and any consent requirements before those services are enabled.

External websites

Links to other websites are provided for convenience and independent education. When you follow an external link, that website's privacy practices apply. Review its privacy notice before sharing information.

Your choices and local requirements

Privacy rights vary by location. If advertising, analytics or other data processing is introduced, the site owner should provide any required disclosures, consent controls and rights-request process for visitors in applicable regions, including the UK, European Economic Area, Australia, Canada and the United States.

Deleting local preferences

You can remove remembered display preferences through your browser's site-data controls. Calculator values are calculated in the page and are not stored by the current static implementation. Clearing browser storage or closing a page does not transmit the entries to FinanceHub.

Contact and updates

Use the contact page for privacy questions or to report an issue. We will update this notice and the date above when data practices or connected services change.
