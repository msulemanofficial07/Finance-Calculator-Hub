Privacy Notice

Last updated: October 10, 2026

This notice describes the current FinanceHub website. Review and update it before adding advertising, analytics, forms, accounts or other data-collection services. Your web host may process technical request logs under its own settings and policies.

The calculator inputs are processed by JavaScript in your browser to display estimates. The current static site does not send calculator values to a FinanceHub server, create an account, or include a contact or lead form.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

The current site does not include analytics, advertising, affiliate tracking or cookie-based measurement scripts. If these or other third-party services are added later, they may collect information through cookies or similar technologies. This notice must be updated to identify providers, purposes, controls and any consent requirements before those services are enabled.

External websites

Links to other websites are provided for convenience and independent education. When you follow an external link, that website's privacy practices apply. Review its privacy notice before sharing information.

Your choices and local requirements

Privacy rights vary by location. If advertising, analytics or other data processing is introduced, the site owner should provide any required disclosures, consent controls and rights-request process for visitors in applicable regions, including the UK, European Economic Area, Australia, Canada and the United States.

Contact and updates

This site has no public contact form at this time. Before launch, publish a monitored contact method for privacy questions and replace this template with a notice reviewed for the site's actual host, operators, services and applicable laws. We will update the date above when the notice changes.
